01 / About Iyunga

A historic school with a technical soul.

From its historic campus to modern science and technical education, Iyunga represents a distinctive tradition of disciplined learning in Tanzania.

Iyunga Technical Secondary School is a government boys' boarding school located in Mbeya City, Tanzania. Its history is associated with a campus constructed in the early twentieth century and a long tradition of secondary and technical education.

Today, the school operates under the public education system and is registered with the National Examinations Council of Tanzania under examination centre S0112.

What makes Iyunga distinctive is the combination of conventional secondary education with technical and engineering-oriented learning. Students encounter areas such as electrical engineering, construction, draughting and other practical disciplines.
